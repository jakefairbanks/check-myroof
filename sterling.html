Storm damage & insurance restoration

Dealing with storm damage? We know the process.

Hail and wind are part of living on the Front Range, and insurance restoration is a big part of what we do. We’re not adjusters and we don’t decide coverage. Our job is to make sure the damage is documented properly, the estimate reflects the work your home actually needs, and the restoration gets done right.

01 Inspect

Find and document the damage

We get on the roof and around the house and photograph what we find: roof, gutters, siding, paint, and screens.

02 Explain

Walk you through it

We show you what we found and whether it’s worth a claim, and explain how the process works and where we fit in. If it isn’t worth filing, we’ll tell you.

03 Review

Check the insurance estimate

We compare your carrier’s estimate to what the repair actually takes, meet the adjuster when it helps, and send documentation for anything that was missed.

04 Restore

Do the work right

We complete the approved repairs with a project supervisor on site, keep you updated as we go, and clean up when we’re done.

Straight answers

Questions homeowners ask us.

What areas do you serve?
Denver Metro and Boulder, from our office in Wheat Ridge. That includes Denver, Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster, Broomfield, Thornton, Aurora, Centennial, Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Parker, Boulder, Longmont, and the communities in between. If you’re not sure, give us a call.
Do you handle smaller repairs, or only full replacements?
Both. We do plenty of leak repairs, partial roof replacements, siding sections, trim work, and gutter fixes. If a repair is the right call, that’s what we’ll recommend.
Are your inspections and estimates really free?
Yes. We’ll look over your home, take photos of anything we find, and give you a straight recommendation and estimate. There’s no obligation.
Can you help if my project involves an insurance claim?
Yes. Insurance restoration is a big part of what we do. We document the damage, can meet your adjuster on-site, review the carrier’s estimate against what the repair actually takes, and send documentation for anything that was missed. Coverage decisions are up to your insurance company, and we can’t guarantee a claim will be approved, but we’ll make sure your home is represented accurately.
What types of roofing and siding do you install?
For roofs: Owens Corning® asphalt shingles (including Class 4 impact-resistant options), standing seam metal, and synthetic shake and slate. For siding: James Hardie® fiber cement and LP® SmartSide®. We also install seamless aluminum gutters and handle exterior painting.
What makes your roofing warranties different?
As an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor, we can offer Owens Corning’s strongest system warranties on eligible roofs, including the Platinum Protection Limited Warranty. It covers materials and includes manufacturer-backed workmanship coverage, so it doesn’t depend only on the contractor. Coverage and eligibility depend on the products installed, so we’ll go over exactly what applies to your roof.
