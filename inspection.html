Straight answers

Questions homeowners ask us after every storm.

Is the inspection really free — what’s the catch?
It’s free, and there’s no obligation. We inspect, photograph, and tell you honestly whether you have damage worth a claim. If you don’t, we’ll say so and leave you with the photos. We only earn anything if you choose us to do the work.
Will filing a claim raise my insurance rates?
In Colorado, hail and wind are “acts of God,” and insurers generally can’t surcharge you individually for a single weather claim the way they would for an at-fault car accident. Rates in hail-prone areas do rise over time for everyone, claim or not. Installing a Class 4 roof may actually earn you a discount — ask your carrier.
What do I actually pay?
Your deductible — that’s it. Insurance pays the rest of the approved scope, and we include the Class 4 Duration FLEX® shingle upgrade and the Platinum Protection warranty at no additional cost. We’ll never ask you to pay for upgrades the scope didn’t cover.
Can you “waive” or “eat” my deductible?
No — and anyone who offers to is breaking Colorado law (SB 38) and putting you at risk of insurance fraud. We give you something better: a legitimately upgraded roof and a warranty backed by Owens Corning, at the same deductible you’d pay anyone else.
How long do I have to file after a storm?
Most Colorado policies give you one year from the date of loss, but some are shorter, and damage gets harder to prove the longer you wait. If a storm hit in the last 12 months, get inspected now.
What if my claim was already denied or underpaid?
That happens more than you’d think — adjusters miss damage, especially on steep or multi-slope roofs. We’ll re-inspect free, document what was missed, and help you request a re-inspection with your carrier.
